About Lesotho

Discover the Mountain Kingdom

Our Story

Lesotho is a landlocked mountain kingdom, known as the "Kingdom in the Sky." At over 1,400 metres above sea level, its highlands shape a resilient culture of herders, weavers, and storytellers. Today we work with local communities to share authentic experiences - from highland treks to cultural homestays - while protecting the landscape and traditions for future generations.

Basotho Culture

The Basotho people have lived in the highlands for generations, known for traditional hospitality, pony culture, and resilience. From the daily life in stone-walled villages to seasonal journeys across the mountains, Lesotho's culture is rooted in community, music, and stewardship of the land. We partner with local guides and weavers to support sustainable tourism that benefits villages in Maseru, Mokhotlong, and Thaba-Tseka.

Quick Facts

  • Area: 30,355 km²
  • Population: ~2.1 million
  • Capital: Maseru
  • Highest Peak: Thabana Ntlenyana - 3,482 m
